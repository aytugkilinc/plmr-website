About PLMR

Independent technical support, led by an engineer.

Work with Aytuğ Kılınç on project solutions, drawings, Türkiye supplier coordination and company-specific product workflows.

The person behind PLMR

Aytuğ Kılınç · Civil Engineer

PLMR is led by Aytuğ Kılınç, a civil engineer based in Istanbul, Türkiye, with more than five years of sector experience. His background includes 2D/3D projects, costing, quotations, production preparation and customer/supplier communication. The work focuses on pergola and roof systems, glazing and screens, and project-specific aluminum or steel components.

The starting point is a practical project question: what needs to fit, what information is missing, which interface needs attention, or what must be clarified before a sourcing decision?

Technical work is most useful when its output and limits are clear. PLMR's approach is to record the available inputs, agree the task and identify the next decision the work is intended to support.

Where PLMR fits into a project

Installers, distributors and project teams can request support with a specific issue or an agreed sequence of technical tasks.

Before system selection

Clarify the intended use, geometry, support conditions and information needed to compare possible routes.

During coordination

Bring roof, glazing, screens, drainage and connection details into a shared record of drawings and open questions.

Before procurement

Prepare technical questions and compare supplier information against the agreed requirement.

Clear roles from the start

PLMR combines technical project services, Türkiye-based supplier coordination and company-specific software workflows. Personal sector experience predates the independent PLMR business. Manufacturing, site measurement, installation, local approvals and product warranty responsibilities must be defined for each project with the parties providing them.

There is no need to assume a complete package of services: start with the question you need answered and agree the output, schedule and commercial terms before work begins.

An independent local partner

Technical research, sourcing and supplier quality support.

PLMR's scope extends across the aluminum sector and associated building materials. It can organize profile and system sourcing, supplier enquiries and technical support for joinery, facade components, composite panels, sandwich panels and related materials.
