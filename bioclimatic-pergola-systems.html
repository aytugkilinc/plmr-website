Bioclimatic / louvered roof

Bioclimatic Pergola Systems

A louvered roof can control the balance between open sky, shade and weather protection, but the quality of the final project depends on more than the louvers.

Send a Project Brief

System overview

What it is

A bioclimatic pergola typically uses adjustable aluminum roof louvers that rotate to regulate sunlight and ventilation. The term describes a system family, not one universal specification; profiles, spans, drainage, controls and side options vary by manufacturer.

Where it can fit

  • Residential terraces
  • Detached outdoor structures
  • Restaurant and café terraces
  • Hotel outdoor areas
  • Commercial hospitality spaces
  • Projects combining roof, glass and screens

Project decisions

Define the technical questions early.

Roof geometry

Rotating louvers, retractable louvers or another roof mechanism?

Structure

Freestanding, wall-mounted or integrated into another structure?

Water

Where does rainfall collect and how is it discharged?

Side enclosures

Will the project use sliding glass, guillotine glass, zip screens or open sides?

Controls

What motor, sensors or control strategy is expected?

Interfaces

How do columns, wall connections, thresholds and adjacent finishes meet?

Technical checklist

Inputs to clarify before selection.

  • Overall width and projection
  • Finished height
  • Structural support condition
  • Column locations
  • Louver direction
  • Drainage outlets
  • Side-enclosure requirement
  • Glass / screen dimensions
  • Motor and control requirements
  • Lighting / accessory requirements
  • Site exposure
  • Maintenance access

FAQ

Is every louvered roof system waterproof?

No universal claim should be made from the category name alone. Water management depends on the selected system, drainage design, installation, interfaces and weather conditions.

Can sliding glass or zip screens be integrated with a bioclimatic pergola?

Often yes, but opening dimensions, guide or track positions, clearances and control strategy should be coordinated with the selected systems.

What is the difference between a bioclimatic roof and a retractable fabric roof?

A louvered roof controls the opening through adjustable blades, while a retractable fabric roof opens through fabric movement. The opening behavior, geometry and drainage logic differ.
