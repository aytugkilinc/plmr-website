Define the component and its manufacturing route

PLMR can help define the requirement, prepare agreed drawing inputs and clarify a possible fabrication route. The project scope must identify the manufacturer, inspection requirements and the parties responsible for ordering, delivery and installation.

Work through the connection using the actual profile geometry

A roof rail may need to meet a different gutter or support profile. PLMR can examine the mating geometry, develop a connection or adapter detail and coordinate the technical requirement with an appropriate fabricator.

Send the actual profiles or drawings, interface dimensions, intended loads or use, material and finish, tolerances, quantity and assembly conditions. The agreed output can include a component drawing, revision record and questions for sample or production review.

The fabricator’s capability, prototype checks, acceptance criteria and release responsibilities are established for the assignment. PLMR’s contribution is technical design and coordination; manufacturing is performed by the agreed producer.

Find a fabrication route for the defined detail

PLMR can research standard sections, custom extrusion or machining routes, fabricated brackets, flashings and compatible accessories to your drawing and material brief. This can be coordinated with architectural systems, composite panels, sandwich panels and other project materials.
