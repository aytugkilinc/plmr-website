PLMR - ILLUSTRATIVE DRAWING AND COORDINATION OUTLINE
Template version: 1.0 | Updated: 14 September 2026
Illustrative example, not a completed client project.
This file is a scope outline, not a drawing. Not for fabrication or construction.

POSSIBLE AGREED OUTPUTS
01 Drawing register: number, title, revision, date, status and intended use.
02 Layout: overall geometry, openings and support locations from agreed inputs.
03 Elevations / sections: interfaces that need coordination.
04 Detail schedule: roof-to-glass, screen-guide, drainage and fixing interfaces.
05 Interface register: supplied assumptions, open questions and owner of each.
06 Revision note: changes made, remaining decisions and distribution status.

INPUTS TO AGREE
Units; coordinate references; verified dimensions; site photos; supplier system
details; required file format; agreed review process; project responsibilities.

HANDOVER QUESTIONS
Is each drawing clearly labelled for its intended use?
Which inputs were verified and by whom?
Which supplier limits or details remain outstanding?
Who confirms support capacity, anchors and project-specific performance?
Who checks local rules and authorises fabrication or installation?
Which revisions and file formats are included in the agreed scope?

BOUNDARY
Deliverables depend on the agreed commission. A coordination drawing is not
automatically a fabrication release, structural verification, regulatory
approval or installation instruction. Do not use this outline as a design.
