COMPARISON RULE
Compare equivalent scopes. A blank item means unconfirmed, not included.
Keep quotations and supporting evidence alongside the comparison. Supplier
statements must be attributed; do not turn them into PLMR guarantees.

BEFORE ORDERING
Agree the purchasing party, payment route, supplier selection authority,
delivery responsibilities and PLMR's role in writing. Any sourcing fee or
other commercial interest must be disclosed as part of the agreed scope.
