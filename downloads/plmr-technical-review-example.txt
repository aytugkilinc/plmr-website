PLMR - ILLUSTRATIVE TECHNICAL REVIEW OUTLINE
Template version: 1.0 | Updated: 14 September 2026
Illustrative example, not a completed client project.
Not for fabrication or construction. No site or supplier has been verified.

Example question: What must be clarified before selecting a terrace roof
with side screens?

1. INPUT REGISTER
Record each sketch, photo, supplier sheet and measurement with its date and
revision. Label dimensions as site-verified, supplied but unverified, or missing.

2. DECISION TO MAKE
State the intended use, desired roof opening behaviour and side protection.
Separate client preferences from constraints that need technical verification.

3. OPEN QUESTIONS
- Support: identify the existing substrate; seek confirmation of fixing
  capacity from the responsible project professional.
- Drainage: show the proposed outlet and downstream route; request the
  system's drainage details and relevant performance information.
- Movement: check roof and screen travel against the proposed openings.
- Controls: identify motor/control requirements and who confirms compatibility.
- Performance: obtain project requirements and the supplier's applicable
  evidence; do not infer a rating from appearance or a generic brochure.

4. ISSUE REGISTER FORMAT
Issue / available evidence / missing input / person to confirm /
effect on decision / status / date or revision.

5. REVIEW OUTPUT
A written findings note, marked-up supplied material where included in scope,
an open-issues list and a next-action list. Record limitations explicitly.

6. RELEASE BOUNDARY
A preliminary review does not constitute a structural design, local approval,
supplier certification or permission to manufacture. Those responsibilities
and any further calculations must be agreed separately.
