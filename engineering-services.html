A clear handover

Agree the output before preparing it.

The brief should identify the decision to be made, available inputs, intended use of the output, file formats, review steps, timing and fees. Drawings and findings should distinguish verified information from assumptions and outstanding questions.

Revisions and any further calculation or site work are included only where agreed. A preliminary review and a fabrication release require different levels of information and responsibility.

Typical coordination questions

  • Which dimensions are verified, and who owns the site measurement?
  • Where do loads and fixings transfer into the existing structure?
  • Where does water discharge after it leaves the roof?
  • Can glazing, screen guides and roof movement work together?
  • Which supplier evidence and local approvals are still required?

Project responsibilities stay explicit.

PLMR provides the technical support agreed for the project. Local statutory approval, structural verification, supplier certification, fabrication release and installation responsibilities must be assigned to the appropriate parties. A coordination document does not automatically fulfil those functions.

For a specific bracket, connection or aluminum/steel part, see custom component support.

Can I start without a complete drawing set?

Yes. Send the information you have and identify what is missing. The initial enquiry can establish whether a useful review is possible and what additional input is needed.

Are structural calculations or local approvals included?

Only a written project scope can establish what is included. Do not assume that drawing coordination includes structural calculations, local sign-off or regulatory approval.

Can existing supplier drawings be reviewed?

They can form part of the agreed review if you are entitled to share them. Include their date, revision and the questions you need answered.

Practical technical capacity

Project solutions and outsourced drawings.
