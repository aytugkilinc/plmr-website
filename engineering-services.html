Technical coordination

Coordinate the interfaces that determine whether the arrangement fits

A roof drawing may look complete while leaving the glass track, screen guide or drainage outlet unresolved. We consider those interfaces together with the supplied support and fixing information, rather than treating each product as a separate drawing.

The review records where dimensions came from, which supplier revisions were used and who needs to confirm the remaining information. Project-specific performance evidence and support verification are requested from the responsible parties.

Scope and handover

Agree what the drawing package is intended to do

Before work begins, we confirm the scope, available inputs, file formats, revision rounds, intended use, timing and fees. The handover distinguishes verified information from supplied assumptions and items awaiting confirmation.

Site measurements, structural verification, local approvals, fabrication release and installation responsibilities are assigned explicitly. A preliminary technical review and a production drawing package have different information and review requirements.

For U.S. projects, inches, feet and millimeters are handled through agreed drawing units and dimension references. The local project team defines the approvals and professional sign-off required for the site.

Review support for U.S. project teams

Getting started

You can start with an incomplete drawing set

Send what you have and identify the missing information. We can discuss whether a useful review is possible, what additional input is needed and which output would help your team proceed.

Supplier drawings can be part of the review when you are entitled to share them. Include their revision, the proposed configuration and the questions you want answered.

Plan the handover your installation team will use

Carry the agreed details into production

For the roof family, the preparation brief brings together drainage outlet directions, connection geometry, roof movement and the electrical requirements shared by the project team. PLMR coordinates this information with the selected manufacturing partner before production details are released.

Give the site team an identifiable assembly package

Installation drawings accompany each roof project. Profile packages use codes linked to the drawings, while prepared connection holes, fittings and socket connections help the site team follow the intended assembly sequence.

The supplier's workshop checks focus on critical assemblies before dispatch. PLMR can coordinate the requested handover information and review outstanding details within the agreed assignment.
