Example 01 · Illustrative

Review the roof and screen interfaces before selecting the configuration

This review identifies what needs to be clarified before selecting a roof and screen configuration.

The starting information includes a client sketch, approximate opening dimensions, site photos and a supplier brochure. None of these inputs is treated as a verified site survey or project-specific performance assessment.

A useful findings note separates issues from assumptions

  • Support and fixings: identify the proposed attachment locations; ask the responsible professional to confirm substrate and fixing capacity.
  • Drainage: request the system's gutter and outlet details; record where the water is intended to go after discharge.
  • Movement and interfaces: identify roof and screen travel paths; request the supplier details needed to check clearances.
  • Performance: list the project requirements and seek evidence applicable to the selected size and configuration.
  • Decision status: record what can be compared now, what remains open and who must provide the missing information.

The handover can include a written review, annotations to supplied material where agreed, an issue register and next actions. It is not a structural calculation, approval or release to manufacture.

Example 02 · Illustrative

Make the drawing handover clear to the next person using it

This assignment defines the documents needed to coordinate the roof, glazing, screens and drainage.

The agreed drawing set can include a drawing register, overall layout, relevant elevations or sections, interface details and an open-issues record. Each item identifies its revision and intended use.

What the handover should make clear

  • Units, drawing references and the origin of dimensions.
  • Verified information, supplied assumptions and unresolved inputs.
  • Which supplier details have been incorporated and their revisions.
  • Who confirms support capacity, performance, local requirements and release status.
  • What changed in the revision and what decision is still required.

The handover can include the agreed drawings and a coordination record in the formats set out in the scope. This outline is not a technical drawing and does not establish that any project is ready for fabrication or installation.

Example 04 · Illustrative, not a completed client project

Resolve a roof rail connection to a different gutter

The assignment develops a rail-to-gutter connection where the standard interfaces do not match.

The detail needs to coordinate the mating profiles, fastening access, roof movement and drainage route.

PLMR can review supplied geometry, develop an adapter or connection detail and prepare questions for the proposed fabricator.

The agreed output can include a component drawing, material/finish and tolerance requirements, a revision record and sample-review questions.

The review still needs to confirm dimensions, applicable loads, material and fastener suitability, manufacturing capability and the agreed acceptance/release responsibilities. This scenario is explanatory and is not a fabrication or construction document.

Example 05 · Illustrative, not an actual inspection record

Record the outstanding order items before dispatch

The assignment supports a buyer working with an existing Turkish supplier before dispatch.

The starting information includes the agreed order, current drawings, scope and accessory list, supplier progress information and available packing documents.

PLMR can follow up drawing revisions and order items, compare the available packing information with the order, and record outstanding questions with their source and date. An agreed visit can add observations and permitted photographs.

The handover can include a progress or visit note, document/packing checklist and outstanding-item register identifying who must respond and the next action.

The record distinguishes documents reviewed from items physically observed. Sampling, quality acceptance, transport and warranty responsibilities are agreed separately; this example is not an accredited inspection or shipment guarantee.
