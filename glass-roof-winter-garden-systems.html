System overview

What it is

Roof geometry, glass specification, drainage, support conditions, side enclosures, ventilation and interfaces all affect the final result. Terminology such as glass roof, veranda and winter garden varies by market; the project requirement matters more than the label.

Where it can fit

  • Fixed glazed roof projects
  • Covered verandas
  • Winter gardens / outdoor rooms
  • Restaurant and café enclosures
  • Residential terrace enclosures
  • Projects combining roof, glass and shading

Project decisions

Define the technical questions early.

Roof form

What geometry and slope are required?

Glazing

What glass build-up and opening strategy apply?

Water

How are gutters and outlets coordinated?

Thermal expectation

Is the space seasonal or expected to behave like conditioned building space?

Side enclosure

Sliding, guillotine, folding or fixed glazing?

Interfaces

How do roof, walls, floor and adjacent construction meet?

Technical checklist

Inputs to clarify before selection.

  • Roof form and slope
  • Support spacing
  • Glass type/build-up
  • Water collection
  • Gutters and outlets
  • Thermal expectations
  • Ventilation
  • Side glazing type
  • Doors/openings
  • Shading
  • Condensation risk
  • Maintenance access

Performance boundary

Use verified product data.

Do not assume wind, snow, water or span performance from the system category alone. Use current documentation for the exact selected product and project requirements.

FAQ

Is a winter garden the same as a veranda?

The terms overlap in some markets. A winter garden generally implies a more enclosed outdoor room, while veranda can describe a roofed transition space.

Can a glass roof be combined with sliding or guillotine glass?

Yes as a concept, but structural, drainage and interface details must be coordinated for the specific systems.

Does PLMR provide structural approval?

PLMR provides technical project support within the agreed scope. Formal structural certification or local engineering approval must be provided by the appropriately licensed professional where required.
