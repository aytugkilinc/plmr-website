System overview

What it is

The term generally describes vertically moving motorized glass panels. Manufacturers use different panel layouts, drive mechanisms, frames, glass configurations, controls and safety details.

Where it can fit

  • Restaurant and café openings
  • Hotel terraces
  • Pergola side enclosures
  • Balcony and outdoor openings
  • Hospitality areas requiring adjustable enclosure
  • Projects where horizontal panel parking is undesirable

FAQ

What is the main difference between guillotine and sliding glass?

Guillotine systems move vertically; sliding systems move horizontally along tracks. The choice affects panel parking, controls, structure and the way the opening is used.

Are all guillotine glass systems motorized?

Commercial systems commonly called automatic guillotine glazing are motorized, but exact mechanisms and configurations vary by manufacturer.

Can a guillotine system be used under a pergola?

It can be a suitable side-enclosure concept when frame connections, heights, controls and weather interfaces are coordinated.
