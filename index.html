FAQ

Before you contact PLMR

What does PLMR provide?

Project-specific technical solutions and drawings, Türkiye-based supplier coordination and configurable product workflows through Automation Studio. Scope and commercial terms are agreed for each assignment.

Does PLMR manufacture or install the systems shown?

The system pages describe the types of projects PLMR supports. Manufacturing and installation are separate responsibilities that must be assigned to the suppliers or project parties providing them.

Can I start before choosing a system?

Yes. Describe the intended use and the decision you need to make. A review can help identify which inputs are needed to compare suitable routes.

What will I receive?

Depending on the agreed scope, outputs can include a findings note, drawing set, coordination record, supplier enquiry brief or comparison. See illustrative examples.

What should I send first?

Send the project location, approximate dimensions, what needs to be resolved and any useful photos, drawings or quotation. Copy or download the project brief.

Can PLMR work with my existing Turkish supplier?

Yes. We can coordinate product questions, orders, agreed factory visits and quality checks with your chosen supplier. Send the supplier, order details and the checks or technical support you need.

Can I ask about profiles, composite panels or sandwich panels?

Yes. PLMR can organize sourcing for these and other aluminum-sector or associated building-material requirements. Share the specification, finish, quantities, destination and preferred brand or supplier.
