System overview

Specify the frame and glazing together.

A thermally broken aluminum system separates its inner and outer metal parts with an insulating barrier. The finished assembly also depends on glazing, seals, opening type, hardware and the perimeter installation detail.

PLMR can support insulated casement and tilt-and-turn window requirements, hinged doors, fixed glazing and related architectural joinery. Share the required configuration and performance documents rather than relying on the product label alone.

From specification to order

Keep the component list coordinated.

  • Series, profile codes, color and finish.
  • Opening direction, leaf sizes and verified dimensions.
  • Insulated glazing and seal specification.
  • Hinges, handles, locking and accessories.
  • Perimeter connections, drainage and order documentation.
