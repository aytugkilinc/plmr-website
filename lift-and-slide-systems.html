Lift & Slide (Hebeschiebe) Doors

Framed sliding doors with lift-and-slide hardware, glazing and threshold details coordinated for the project.

Discuss Your Requirement

System overview

Lift the sash, then slide it.

Hebeschiebe is the German term commonly used for lift-and-slide door systems. Operating the handle lifts the moving sash from its sealed position so it can travel horizontally; lowering it engages the system's closing and sealing arrangement.

These framed architectural doors are a different selection route from lightweight terrace sliding glass. Thermally broken profiles, insulated glazing and the appropriate hardware can be specified through the selected system house.

Project inputs

Define the complete door configuration.

  • Opening size, units and fixed/moving leaf arrangement.
  • Profile series, finish and glazing specification.
  • Threshold, drainage and accessibility requirements.
  • Hardware, handle, locking and sash weight.
  • Project location and requested performance documents.
