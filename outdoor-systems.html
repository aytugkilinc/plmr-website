Selection logic

The product name is not the starting point.

Before comparing systems, define the intended use, project location, opening dimensions, structural condition, desired amount of opening, rain and drainage strategy, side enclosure, solar control and automation requirements.

  • Intended use
  • Opening dimensions
  • Wall-mounted or freestanding condition
  • Desired roof opening
  • Drainage route
  • Glass / screen requirement
  • Controls / automation
  • Available drawings and site information
