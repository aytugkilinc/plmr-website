How to Choose Side Enclosures for a Pergola

Once a project adds side protection, the design becomes an integrated roof-and-enclosure system. The correct route depends on what the side is expected to do.

PLMR Technical InsightsUpdated 10 Sep 2026

1. Define the purpose

Start with the problem: wind reduction, rain protection, solar control, privacy, seasonal enclosure, visual transparency, motorized opening or maximum clear opening. One product rarely maximizes every goal.

2. Sliding glass

Use where horizontal panel movement and panel parking are acceptable. Review rail count, parking side, threshold, corner conditions, clear opening, glass specification and maintenance.

3. Guillotine glass

Use where vertical motorized movement fits the architectural and operational requirement. Review panel arrangement, opening heights, controls, safety and service access.

4. Zip screens

Useful for solar control, privacy and visual filtering. They require head/cassette space, side-guide fixing, suitable dimensions, power/control and verified exposure limits.

5. Combining glass and screens

Combined systems can serve different functions but increase interface complexity. Coordinate track depth, guide depth, beam/column space, drainage, electrical route and service access.

6. Do not forget the roof structure

If side systems are expected, consider them before columns, beam covers, lighting, drainage routes and finishes are finalized.
