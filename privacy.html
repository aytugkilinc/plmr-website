Privacy

Privacy and project enquiries

How this website and project correspondence relate to your information.

Last updated: 8 October 2026.

Contact responsible for PLMR enquiries

For questions about information shared with PLMR, contact Aytuğ Kılınç at aytugkilinc@plmrsolutions.com.

Using the contact form or project brief

The contact page provides a project enquiry form as well as an email link and a blank brief that you can copy or download. The form can include your name, email address, company, country, project information, reference images and file-sharing links that you choose to provide. This information is used to understand and respond to your enquiry and, where a project is agreed, to discuss and carry out that work.

Direct form delivery is handled through the website infrastructure. If direct delivery is unavailable, the page can offer an email draft instead; the site does not claim that an enquiry or image was sent unless the server confirms delivery. Copying or downloading the blank brief does not send an enquiry.

When you email PLMR directly, your message may contain your name, contact details, company or project information, attachments and any file links you choose to share. You decide what to include and when to send it.

Contacting PLMR on WhatsApp

WhatsApp links open a conversation with PLMR and a prepared introduction. You choose whether to edit and send it. The website does not load an embedded WhatsApp chat or send a message on your behalf. Your use of WhatsApp is subject to that service’s own practices.

Information relevant to the work

Include only the information needed for your request and files you are entitled to share. Avoid passwords, identity documents and unrelated personal or confidential information. For sensitive project material, discuss an appropriate sharing method before sending it.

If a project requires information to be passed to a supplier or another project participant, the recipient, information and purpose should be agreed as part of the work. Limit access to shared file links to the intended recipients.

Hosting, form delivery, email and technical data

The website is hosted through Cloudflare. Loading a page or submitting the contact form involves technical request information such as network addresses, requested resources, browser information and timestamps. Hosting and delivery services may process technical data for delivery, security and service operation.

Reference images submitted through the form are processed only as part of the enquiry delivery flow. Large project files are not uploaded through this form; use the project link field for Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, WeTransfer, SharePoint or another sharing service. Those providers process files under their own practices and access settings.

Email and file-sharing providers process correspondence or files through their own services. Your use of an external email application or shared-file service is also subject to that provider's practices.

The brief-copying feature does not require a tracking cookie. Hosting services may apply separate security or performance measurements. Questions about those services or site measurements can be sent to the contact above.

Keeping information and handling requests

Project correspondence may need to be retained for enquiry follow-up, agreed work and applicable record-keeping obligations. Retention depends on the purpose and circumstances; contact PLMR about a specific record.

You can contact PLMR to ask what information has been received, request correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern. The response will depend on the request and applicable obligations. Please identify the correspondence concerned without sending unnecessary identity documents.

Updates

This notice should be reviewed when the website's enquiry method, measurement tools or project processes change.

Software customer information and separate activities

Software customer or dealer details, projects, quotations, prices and commercial relationships are not used to support PLMR’s separate sourcing or product-supply activity.

The actual software implementation agreement defines data handling, confidentiality, permissions and authorized support access.
