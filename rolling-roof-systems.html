One roof family · Project-specific mechanisms

Rolling / Retractable Roof Systems

Roof panels that retract to open the space, with the movement, parking zone, supports and drainage coordinated from the start.

Discuss Your Roof Project

One product route

Plan the open and closed roof positions.

PLMR groups rolling and retractable roof enquiries in one system family. The exact panel arrangement and operating sequence depend on the selected manufacturer.

Rigid aluminum panels or louvers can travel and collect to uncover the opening. Define the opening area, parking space, guide rails, drives, service access and drainage in both positions.

PLMR support

Turn the mechanism into a workable layout.

We can compare roof arrangements, coordinate glazing and screens, develop agreed layouts and connection details, and clarify the selected supplier's system information.

Send dimensions and units, support positions, the desired opening behavior and available drawings or site photographs.

Fabric pergola option

A guided fabric canopy for the terrace.

For fabric or PVC pergolas, the flexible covering gathers along guide rails and transverse support bars. Its roof geometry and water strategy differ from a retracting rigid-panel roof.

Both are discussed within the retractable-roof route, with the actual mechanism and material kept explicit in the enquiry. Compare the selected system's configuration, operating limits and supporting documents.

Movement & parking

Travel direction, panel collection space, clear opening and access to guides and drives.

Roof & enclosure interfaces

Supports, wall connections, glazing, screens and the clearance each moving element needs.

Drainage & operation

Gutters, outlets, seals, controls and supplier instructions for the selected arrangement.

Different settings, one roof family

From city restaurants to residential terraces.

A retractable roof can be considered for a compact courtyard, a rooftop restaurant or a residential terrace. The opening geometry, support locations and roof-panel parking area guide the selected configuration.

Share the site layout and the opening you want to achieve so PLMR can coordinate the roof and enclosure requirements with the supplier.
