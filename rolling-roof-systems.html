Retractable panel roofs

Retractable Rolling Roof Systems

A moving-panel roof needs space to open, room for its mechanism and a coordinated route for water. Begin with the operating behavior required for the project.

Discuss Your Roof Project

System overview

Understand how the roof opens.

“Rolling roof” is used for retractable roof-panel arrangements whose panels travel or collect to expose part of the opening. The mechanism, panel layout, stacking area and drainage details depend on the manufacturer.

A tilting louvered pergola changes blade angle for light and ventilation. A retractable fabric pergola gathers its flexible covering. Some panel systems combine movements, so compare the actual operating sequence and clear opening rather than the product label.

Where it can fit

Plan the open and closed positions.

Restaurant terraces, commercial outdoor areas and patios may call for a roof that opens beyond blade rotation. The opening target, supporting structure and side enclosures determine which arrangement is worth reviewing.

This page explains the project considerations and PLMR’s support. A supplier’s exact product data determines the available configuration and documented performance.

Project decisions

Coordinate movement, supports and water.

Movement and parking

Identify travel direction, the panel collection or stack area, the clear opening in each operating position and service access to drives and guides.

Structure and interfaces

Locate supports, wall connections and guide positions. Check that glazing, screens, gutters and adjacent construction do not obstruct movement.

Drainage and operation

Request the selected system’s gutter, outlet, seal, motor and control details. Clarify permitted operating conditions and the maintenance information needed.

Match the evidence to the configuration.

Use current supplier documentation for the proposed size, roof geometry and project conditions. General category descriptions do not establish span, wind, snow, water or structural performance.

The project scope identifies who confirms measurements, design loads, support capacity, local approvals and release for fabrication or installation.
