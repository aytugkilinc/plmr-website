How Suphan Glide works.

Suphan Glide retracts the aluminum roof into a collected position. The guided panel movement changes how much of the roof area is open, giving you a choice between a covered layout and an opening to the sky.

Suphan Glide is retract-only. Panel angles in the catalog illustrate stages of the collection mechanism; they are not a separate user-selectable tilt function. For both tilt and retract, choose Zagro Motion.
