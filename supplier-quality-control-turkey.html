Supplier visits · Order checks · Reporting

Supplier Quality Control in Türkiye

Your local partner for agreed factory visits, product checks and pre-dispatch follow-up with new or existing Turkish suppliers.

Discuss a Supplier Check

Work through your own supplier

We can act locally on your behalf.

You do not need to change manufacturers. PLMR can communicate with your chosen supplier, follow up drawing revisions, visit the agreed location and check accessible products against your order and inspection brief.

Support can cover profiles, windows and doors, pergola and glazing components, composite panels, sandwich panels and associated materials. The visit stage and checks are tailored to the actual order.

Agree before the visit

Send the basis for the check.

  • Supplier, factory location and contact person.
  • Order, approved drawings and current revisions.
  • Product and accessory list, quantities and finish.
  • Dimensions, tolerances and acceptance criteria.
  • Production stage, dispatch date and priority concerns.

Practical checks, clear observations.

Product & dimension checks

Compare sampled accessible dimensions and product identity with the supplied drawings, profile codes and agreed requirements.

Finish & condition

Record visible coating or surface issues, damage, color differences and workmanship observations against agreed reference samples.

Hardware & accessories

Check listed seals, fixings, mechanisms, motors or accessories; observe agreed operation checks when practical.

Quantity & packing

Compare available item counts, labels, lengths, packing lists and protection with the agreed order and shipment requirements.

Documents & revisions

Follow up current drawings, supplier material or test documents, approved changes and outstanding technical questions.

Corrective-action follow-up

Record findings with permitted photographs, responsible parties and next actions; follow up the agreed corrections.

The report you receive

Know what was checked and what remains open.

The handover can include a dated visit report, sampled measurements, photographic record, document checklist and an outstanding-item register. It identifies the items observed, sampling scope, inaccessible items and the supplier's responses.

Visits, access, equipment, sampling and reporting are agreed in advance. Laboratory testing or accredited certification, when required, is arranged through suitable providers as a separate scope.
