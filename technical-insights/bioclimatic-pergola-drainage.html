Plan Where Water Goes After It Leaves the Pergola Roof

Review the gutter, outlet and site discharge route together when preparing a louvered pergola or patio roof enquiry.

PLMR Technical InsightsUpdated 11 October 2026

Follow the water beyond the roof drawing

A roof drawing can show a gutter without explaining where the water goes after the outlet. The project review needs the complete route from the selected roof arrangement to the proposed site discharge location.

Begin with the technical details for the selected product. Identify the roof position, collection features, perimeter gutters and outlet arrangement shown for that configuration. Request any detail missing from the technical enquiry.

Review gutters and outlets against the proposed frame

Ask the supplier to show how the gutter connects to the roof profiles and where outlets occur within the modules. Record the proposed direction of flow and how joined modules or changes in geometry affect the arrangement.

For a louvered pergola, retractable aluminum roof or fabric roof, use the documented product details. Similar-looking frames do not establish an identical drainage design.

Coordinate the discharge with the surrounding construction

Show outlet locations alongside columns, glass tracks, screen guides, floor finishes and nearby building elements. The route after discharge should be defined by the project team, including the relevant site levels and connection conditions.

Glass or screen details can occupy the same space as a proposed outlet or maintenance opening. Review those interfaces before fixing the enclosure layout.

Keep performance statements tied to evidence

A drainage illustration explains an arrangement; it does not establish a tested capacity or weather rating. Request the product information and evidence the responsible project professionals need for the intended configuration and site.

Record missing information explicitly. Do not infer zero pitch from a flat architectural appearance or apply a performance statement from another system to the proposed roof.

Turn the questions into a defined technical review

Share the roof layout, gutter and outlet details, site photographs and proposed enclosure. PLMR can review the supplied information, annotate the interfaces and record the questions to be answered by the supplier or project team.

The engineering and drawing scope identifies the intended output and review responsibilities. Further calculations, site verification and approvals are included only where expressly agreed.
