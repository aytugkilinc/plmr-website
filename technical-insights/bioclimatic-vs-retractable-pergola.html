Choose Between a Louvered Pergola and a Retractable Roof

Compare roof movement, open sky, drainage and enclosure interfaces before selecting the configuration for your project.

PLMR Technical InsightsUpdated 11 October 2026

Choose the movement around the intended use

A louvered pergola changes the angle of its aluminum roof panels. A retractable roof collects its covering to open part of the roof area. Some systems provide both movements, so the category name alone is not enough to define the configuration.

Start with what the user needs to control. Tilting panels adjust the roof position without collecting it, while a retracting system needs a defined collection area and travel path. Request the supplier’s movement sequence for the selected arrangement.

Compare the documented roof options

Artos Tilt and Teton Eco Tilt tilt in place. Their panel sections and pivot details differ. Zagro Motion combines tilt and retract, while Suphan Glide retracts only.

Fabric roofs are a separate route. Antero Pergola has a flat roof appearance and retractable fabric. The inclined Fabric Pergola remains a distinct option. A flat appearance does not establish zero fabric pitch or a drainage rating.

Review the frame and side enclosures with the roof

Show the proposed supports, clear heights, glass panels and screen guides on the same layout. Roof movement and service access need to be considered alongside the adjoining enclosure.

Request the selected profile and connection information rather than assuming that products sharing a general frame concept fit every combination. Mixed moving and fixed roof layouts are reviewed through the project-specific Meru Flex approach.

Ask for the information needed to compare the proposals

Give each supplier the same dimensions, units, mounting condition and intended use. Ask for its proposed configuration, relevant product documents and included components. The responsible project team identifies the site-specific review and evidence required.

A comparison can then record the roof movement, panel arrangement, drainage route, control scope and unanswered questions against the brief. PLMR’s technical review can help prepare that record before procurement progresses.
