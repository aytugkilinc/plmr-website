Give the Supplier a Clear Brief for Your Commercial Patio Enclosure

Prepare the roof, glass opening and screen requirements together for a restaurant, hotel or commercial terrace supplier enquiry.

Written by Aytuğ Kılınç, Civil Engineer11 October 2026

Explain how the space will be used

A restaurant patio, hotel terrace and covered waiting area can have different opening and enclosure requirements. Describe the intended use, when the space should open and which boundaries need to remain in place. Include the project location and target stage.

Panoramic glazing describes the visual intent, but it does not specify how the glass operates. Define whether panels should move vertically, slide horizontally or remain fixed, then ask for the actual frame and glazing arrangement.

Show the opening and the surrounding construction

Share plans, elevations, sections and site photographs if available. Record width, height, roof projection and drawing units, together with the source of each dimension. Identify proposed supports and existing conditions that may affect connections.

A drawing should show floor levels, roof or beam positions and the spaces needed for movement. Indicate which dimensions are still preliminary so the supplier does not treat them as confirmed order inputs.

Choose the glass movement around the available space

Vertical retractable glass walls collect their moving panels vertically. Sliding glass walls use horizontal travel and panel parking. Both need a defined relationship to the supporting frame, sill and adjoining finishes.

Ask the supplier to show the clear opening, panel collection and operating sequence for the proposed arrangement. If a remaining glass panel is intended to serve a boundary function, request the applicable product information for review rather than assuming it from the appearance.

Coordinate the roof and screens in the same layout

The roof movement, glass panels and motorized exterior screens need their own travel paths and service access. Identify the head space for cassettes, guide fixing surfaces and the route from roof outlets to the site drainage arrangement.

Provide a combined layout or request one within the drawing scope. A shared frame concept does not establish that every proposed component can be fitted without checking its interfaces.

Ask for a proposal that your team can review

Request drawings, a component schedule, the relevant product documents and a written delivery scope. Your local project professionals should identify the approval and review requirements for the site.
