Coordinate the Pergola Roof with Glass Enclosures and Screens

Plan sliding glass, vertical retractable glass and ZIP screens around the roof, supports and available opening.

PLMR Technical InsightsUpdated 11 October 2026

Define the enclosure before fixing the roof layout

Sliding glass, vertical retractable glass and ZIP screens each need space within or alongside the pergola frame. Describe the opening, intended use and operating sequence before the roof dimensions and support positions are finalized.

Include the desired view, shade or enclosure in the brief, then identify the product arrangement that is being proposed. The selected supplier details establish the actual component geometry and movement.

Plan where the glass panels move and collect

Sliding glass walls need tracks and horizontal panel parking. Vertical retractable glass walls need a defined vertical collection arrangement, drive and control scope.

Request the clear opening, frame dimensions and service-access details. A light terrace enclosure and an insulated patio door should be evaluated through their own glazing and component specifications.

Give screens a defined head and guide arrangement

Motorized exterior screens require head space for the cassette and surfaces for the side-guide fixings. Coordinate the screen travel with adjoining glass panels and roof movement.

Specify the fabric and operating requirements through the selected system. If glass and screens are proposed for the same opening, show their positions and travel paths together rather than assuming a standard combined detail.

Coordinate connections, thresholds and drainage

Bring the roof beam, column, glass track, screen guide and floor finish into the same review. Identify where water is intended to discharge and which details require confirmation from the supplier or local project team.

Mixed systems and the Meru Flex configuration approach are project-specific. A shared frame concept is a starting point for the review, not confirmation of every proposed combination.

Ask for a drawing package that records the interfaces

Share the plans, elevations, approximate dimensions and supplier details already available. Mark preliminary inputs and state the working units. The handover should identify the revisions used and the questions still awaiting an answer.

PLMR’s technical drawing support can coordinate the agreed layout and connection information. Review the illustrative handover format before defining the output your team needs.
