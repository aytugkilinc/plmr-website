Define the Checks Before Your Outdoor-System Order Leaves Türkiye

Agree the order references, inspection priorities and reporting scope before arranging supplier checks or a factory visit.

Written by Aytuğ Kılınç, Civil Engineer11 October 2026

Start with the current order documents

A useful pre-dispatch check needs an identifiable order, current drawing revisions and the component schedule. Share quantities, finishes, approved changes, accessory lists and the supplier’s current production or packing status.

Describe the concerns that prompted the visit. The checks for aluminum profiles, a pergola assembly, glass components and motorized screens will differ, as will the access available at each production stage.

Agree the checks that can be performed at the visit

The brief can include accessible dimensions, visible finish and condition, component identity, accessories, item counts and available packing information. Operation checks may be included where equipment, access and the production stage allow them.

Define the sampling, reference drawings, tolerances or acceptance criteria supplied for the check. Record what cannot be inspected after packing or what would require equipment, testing or a separate technical scope.

Distinguish documents from physical observations

A supplier may provide a packing list, progress photograph or product document before the visit. The report should identify that source separately from observations made at the factory.

A dated record can include permitted photographs, sampled measurements and an outstanding-item register. It should identify inaccessible items and the questions that still need a supplier response.

Agree the follow-up for unresolved items

Record who needs to respond, which correction is proposed and how the response will be reviewed. If a further visit or sample check is necessary, establish that scope separately rather than implying it is included in the first inspection.

Product acceptance, shipment authorization, transport and warranty responsibilities remain with the parties defined for the order. A supplier visit reports the agreed checks; its scope does not automatically include laboratory testing or accredited certification.

Use local support with your existing supplier

You can commission PLMR to work with the Turkish manufacturer or distributor already supplying your order. Share the factory location, contact, current order and target dispatch date so access and the inspection brief can be discussed.
