What to Check Before Sourcing a Louvered Pergola from Türkiye

Prepare the roof configuration, project inputs and supplier questions before comparing pergola quotations for a U.S. project.

Written by Aytuğ Kılınç, Civil Engineer11 October 2026

Start with the movement your project needs

Motorized louvered pergola describes a system family, not a complete specification. Establish whether the roof should tilt, retract or do both. A rotating panel arrangement and a collecting roof need different movement and clearance information.

Artos Tilt and Teton Eco Tilt tilt in place. Zagro Motion provides tilt and retract, while Suphan Glide retracts only. Their documented profile and mechanism details are the starting point for a project enquiry.

Give each supplier the same project information

Share the city and state, opening dimensions, drawing units, proposed supports and intended use. Mark approximate dimensions clearly and identify who will confirm them. Add the glass walls, patio doors or exterior screens that need to fit within or alongside the frame.

The enquiry should define the roof arrangement, number of modules, finish, accessories, control requirements and the drawings or documents your project team needs. Ask the supplier to identify its proposed configuration and any exclusions.

Compare the evidence as well as the price

A general brochure statement may describe a different size or support arrangement. Request the source documents relevant to the proposed product and ask your responsible project professionals which evidence they need for review. Record missing information as an open question.

Compare included profiles, roof panels, hardware, motors, controls and any side enclosures against the same schedule. Packing, freight and installation responsibilities also need to be visible in the commercial comparison.

Define the drawing and order review sequence

Agree which supplier drawing will be reviewed, how revisions will be recorded and who authorizes the next stage. Confirm how dimensions, finishes and accessories appear in the order documents before procurement progresses.

For an order already in production, an agreed supplier check in Türkiye can add observations and a record of outstanding items. The inspection brief should identify the checks that can be performed at that stage.
