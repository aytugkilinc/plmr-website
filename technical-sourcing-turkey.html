Turn a sourcing request into a comparable technical brief.

Supplier search and technical clarification for outdoor systems and project-specific components from Türkiye.

Start with what the project needs.

A product name alone rarely defines a suitable offer. Dimensions, configuration, performance requirements, interfaces, controls, documentation and delivery scope can change what is actually being compared.

PLMR can support supplier and product search, preparation of technical questions, review of supplied information and comparison against an agreed brief. Support may concern a complete system or a specific aluminum or steel component.

Send these inputs first

  • System or component, intended use and destination.
  • Approximate dimensions, quantities and units.
  • Drawings, photos or an existing supplier offer.
  • Required performance and documentation, if known.
  • Target date and the decision you need to make.

Agree the commercial route before ordering.

The project agreement must identify PLMR's role, fees and any commercial interest, who buys from the supplier and who is responsible for payment, inspection, freight, customs, installation and warranty obligations.

Supplier quotations, stated lead times and product information are assessed within their stated scope and date. A comparison does not turn supplier claims into verified project performance or a PLMR guarantee.

Your existing supplier is a valid starting point

Supplier coordination and order follow-up in Türkiye.

You do not need to change suppliers to request support. PLMR can help clarify technical questions with the Turkish manufacturer you already use, track agreed revisions and record outstanding product or order information.

Where agreed and practical, support can include supplier visits, progress notes, permitted photographs, document review and pre-dispatch checks of supplied packing information, profiles, accessories and outstanding items. Visit location, access, timing, cost and reporting are defined in the scope.

Possible outputs include a supplier question list, visit or follow-up notes, a document and packing checklist, and a record of open items with responsible parties and next actions. These checks describe the information reviewed; shipment acceptance and manufacturing quality responsibilities remain explicit.

Selected supply and export enquiries

Selected outdoor systems, components and ready-to-assemble roof systems can be discussed as a potential supply requirement. Product availability, supplier arrangement, contractual seller, destination, delivery and warranty terms must be established for the particular order.

Technical advice, order coordination, intermediation and acting as a seller are different commercial roles. PLMR’s role and any commercial interest are made clear before an engagement or order.
