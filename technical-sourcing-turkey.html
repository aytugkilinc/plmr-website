Choose the supplier against a defined technical requirement

PLMR is your Türkiye-based technical sourcing partner for outdoor systems, architectural aluminum and associated building materials. We help turn the requirement into comparable supplier information and an agreed order route.

We organize the route from requirement to supplier

Bring a system specification, material schedule or component drawing. We research suitable manufacturing and supply routes, collect the relevant technical information and compare offers against the same requirement. You can nominate your existing Turkish supplier or request alternatives.

Our scope spans pergolas and glazing, Hebeschiebe and insulated joinery, aluminum profiles, sheet and coil, facade components, composite panels, sandwich panels and related building materials.
