How Teton Eco Tilt works

Teton Eco Tilt rotates its S-shaped aluminum panels within the frame. Adjust the angle for daylight and airflow, or return the panels to their closed position.

Teton uses a 228.2 mm S panel, while Artos Tilt offers a 240 mm panel and a separate 380 × 45 mm insulated panel option. Both systems tilt; compare their panel sections and pivot arrangements when defining the roof.

Sections & hardware

Inside Teton Eco Tilt

The S-panel pivots connect to prepared support profiles. Drilled and notched connection details locate the tilt components within the supporting arrangement.

The connection details matter as much as the roof

Steel connections with a protective finish

Connection parts use 6, 8 or 10 mm ST37 steel according to their position and function. Cataphoresis coating provides corrosion protection for these steel components.

Fasteners selected for the connection

Bolts and pins use stainless steel or a galvanized finish with an additional chrome coating, as specified for the connection. Fastener dimensions and thread engagement are selected for the required tightening conditions and system alignment.

Resolve drainage and electrical details before production

Plan where the water leaves the system

Drainage outlet locations and directions are agreed during project development and incorporated into production. Share the site drainage route before the details are released so the outlet arrangement can be coordinated with the surrounding work.

Prepare the motor and lighting connections

Motor and LED connections use control units with socket connections for the system components. When the electrical requirements are shared during project development, the relevant assemblies can be prepared in the workshop to simplify installation.

The selected motor, control unit and power requirements are identified for the project. Site power provision is coordinated with the electrical installer.

Coordinate the sides with the roof

Choose open sides or discuss the glazing, screens and doors your space needs. Interfaces are reviewed with the selected roof and supporting layout.

Choose a finish that belongs in the architecture

Choose the powder coating for the project

Powder coating options include Class 1 standard durability and Class 2 superdurable finishes. These coating classes concern weathering performance, including color and gloss retention. The coating product, class and finish are specified for the project.

Match the finish to the architecture

Wood-effect transfer finishes offer another way to coordinate the system with its surroundings. Other coating and finish requirements can be discussed on request, with the proposed process and sample agreed before production.

Prepare the system before it reaches the site

Critical details checked before dispatch

Key connection and mechanism assemblies are trial fitted and checked in the manufacturing workshop before shipment. These checks focus on the important details that need to fit and operate together.

Connections prepared for the assembly sequence

Internal connection holes and the specified fittings are prepared before dispatch. Louver and scissor arrangements are set out to reduce site preparation and support the planned assembly sequence.

Sealant is used only at the designated technical details within the system. Follow the specified assembly and sealing points for the selected configuration.

Know the contents before opening the package

Profile packages carry identification codes that correspond to the accompanying project drawings. The installation team can identify the contents without opening every package. Crates follow a defined packing layout for the supplied system.

Installation drawings travel with the project

Each project is supplied with installation drawings. Package codes, prepared connection details and the drawing sequence give the site team a clear reference for assembly.

PLMR coordinates the technical requirements and handover with the selected manufacturing partner. Appearance, connection design and assembly access are considered together, with feedback used to refine the details.
