Technical sourcing

Define the system before asking suppliers to price it

A product brochure is a starting point. Your procurement brief also needs the proposed dimensions, opening arrangement, profile details, accessories and documents required by the project team. PLMR helps organize that information before comparing the supplier routes.

Based in Istanbul, we can work with your existing Turkish manufacturer or research options against the brief. The assignment can cover a technical review, drawing coordination, supplier comparison or agreed local order follow-up.

Review tilt-only, tilt-and-retract and retract-only aluminum roofs as distinct options. Retractable fabric pergolas and fixed glass or sandwich panel roofs are separate selection routes.

What PLMR can prepare

Give your project team a comparison it can use

PLMR can prepare a supplier enquiry brief, compare quotations against the same scope and record the missing technical information. Drawing support can clarify layouts and component interfaces using the dimensions and supplier revisions supplied for the assignment.

An agreed supplier visit or product check can add dated observations, permitted photographs and an outstanding-item record before dispatch. The report identifies the items checked and the limits of the visit.

Project coordination

Keep the drawing units and project responsibilities explicit

State whether your drawings and dimensions use feet, inches or millimeters. We agree the working units and dimension references, including which measurements are verified and which are preliminary.

Your U.S. project professionals define the required local review, permits and approvals. PLMR coordinates the requested supplier information; product acceptance is reviewed for the particular U.S. site and configuration.

Manufacturing, shipping, installation and warranty responsibilities are identified with the parties providing them. PLMR’s technical services, supplier coordination and any supply arrangements are agreed for the assignment.

Preparing the enquiry

Start with the brief or quotation you already have

Send your company and role, project city and state, intended system, approximate dimensions and drawing units. Include any supplier quotation, product documents or drawings you are entitled to share, together with the decision and target date.

If the supplier is already selected, tell us what needs to be clarified or checked. If it is not, describe the technical requirement and the basis on which you want options compared.
