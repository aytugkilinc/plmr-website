How Zagro Motion works.

Zagro Motion combines two roof movements: panels tilt around their axes and the roof retracts along its guides. Choose an angled roof position for daylight and air, or collect the panels to create an open roof area.

Zagro Motion is the tilt-and-retract choice. Suphan Glide is the retract-only aluminum roof; its collection mechanism does not create a separate user-selectable tilt function.
